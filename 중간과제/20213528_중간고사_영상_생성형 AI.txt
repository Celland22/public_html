SCENE AND VISUAL FIDELITY
Preserve the image's dark navy circuit board, realistic silver-blue
semiconductor package positioned on the right, macro photography angle,
fine PCB traces, and clean negative space on the left. Use physically
plausible surfaces and camera motion, not science-fiction transformations.

ANIMATION TIMELINE
0.0–1.0 s — Begin with the semiconductor chip and its PCB under calm,
subtle, low-key blue studio lighting. No jump cut.
1.0–3.0 s — Introduce an extremely slow, smooth camera push-in with a small
change of framing. Keep the semiconductor rigid and precisely detailed.
3.0–5.2 s — Add restrained cyan and cobalt-blue data pulses running along
several EXISTING circuit traces. Let the chip remain sharp and stable.
5.2–6.7 s — Add a natural, gentle cool light sweep across the metal edges,
emphasizing precision packaging and advanced memory technology.
6.7–8.0 s — Ease the camera movement to a nearly stationary hero shot,
with room on the left for a concluding brand message.

VISUAL STYLE
Institutional financial research, dark navy and cobalt blue,
photorealistic macro cinematography, crisp engineered materials,
soft cinematic depth of field, smooth restrained movement, high contrast,
professional lighting, polished but understated technology imagery.

EXCLUSIONS
No rapid camera moves, shaking, abrupt cuts, surreal morphing,
unphysical chip movement, people, floating holograms, financial return
promises, speculative stock charts, coins, fantasy effects, distracting
color transitions, or unrelated visual elements.

OUTPUT
One coherent 8-second horizontal 16:9 corporate investment-research clip,
at the highest available quality, suitable for MP4 export.
