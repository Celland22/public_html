02. 프롬프트 ① — 메인 이미지 생성 (재구성 / English)

ROLE
You are a world-class commercial technology photographer and an art director
specializing in premium institutional equity research publications.

TASK
Create one photorealistic, visually restrained, premium-quality cover image
for an academic Korean semiconductor investment outlook report published
under the fictional research brand "Hallym Investment".

The same image will be used for:
(1) the opening page of an editable Microsoft Word investment report,
(2) the cover slide of a 16:9 PowerPoint presentation,
(3) the first visual reference for an eight-second semiconductor promotional video.

SUBJECT AND PHYSICAL REALISM
Depict a credible advanced memory semiconductor package resting on a dark,
realistic multilayer printed circuit board. The main package consists of
refined metallic silver and cool-blue semiconductor surfaces, precisely
engineered layered edges, densely routed copper traces, tiny components,
fine pin structures, subtle microscopic surface detail, and studio-quality
materials. Make the object believable as modern semiconductor hardware;
do not depict a magical glowing cube or an unrecognizable science-fiction prop.

COMPOSITION
Use a cinematic horizontal 16:9 composition. Position the main semiconductor
slightly right of center, around two thirds across the frame, viewed from a
subtle elevated three-quarter angle. The right section should contain the
most visually detailed parts of the chip and circuit board. Reserve at least
the left 35–40% as clean, gently graded, dark negative space for a title that
will later be added as editable text in Word and PowerPoint.

LIGHTING AND COLOR
Use deep midnight navy (#0D1B33), near-black (#07101F), controlled cobalt
blue (#2777D6), muted cyan (#44BCED), and realistic brushed silver.
Light the upper metal surfaces with soft edge reflections and balanced cool
studio highlights. Retain a cinematic low-key atmosphere without making the
hardware excessively luminous or unrealistic. Keep the semiconductor details
sharp while allowing a gentle background depth-of-field falloff.

VISUAL TONE
Elegant, authoritative, understated, sophisticated, technically precise,
and appropriate for a professional institutional equity research cover.
The viewer should perceive advanced Korean memory semiconductor technology
and analytical credibility rather than a science-fiction action scene.

MOTION-FRIENDLY COMPOSITION
Design the chip and adjacent PCB traces so a video model can later perform
a subtle push-in and animate faint light pulses along EXISTING circuit paths
without disturbing the architecture. Avoid floating particles or elements
that would make image-to-video consistency difficult.

STRICT NEGATIVE REQUIREMENTS
No lettering, captions, numbers, company names, logos, trademarks,
watermarks, invented device labels, financial tickers, stock arrows,
candlestick charts, coins, banknotes, human hands, people, flags, city
skyline, distorted pins, melting structures, unphysical wire routing,
neon overload, purple haze, comic style, or advertisements.
Do not insert the words Hallym Investment in the image itself.

OUTPUT
Produce one clean, ultra-detailed, high-resolution, photorealistic image
suitable for saving as PNG. No presentation mockup, no surrounding borders,
no explanatory text, no multiple design options.

03. 프롬프트 ② — 후속 이미지 수정용 요청 예시 (재구성 / English)

Please refine the image into a cleaner institutional report-cover composition.
Keep the same chip geometry and three-quarter camera angle, but ensure that
at least 35–40% of the LEFT side remains unobstructed and dark enough for
white Korean title text added afterward. Reduce unrealistic neon bloom,
retain crisp metallic layering, emphasize physically plausible PCB traces,
and ensure the final appearance is suitable for an investment analysis
publication instead of a speculative sci-fi poster.
Do not generate any text or logos in the pixels. Return a single final PNG.
